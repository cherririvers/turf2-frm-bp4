## Sports Section

### Section Header
**Title:** Choose Your Game

**Subtitle:** From football to pickleball, we offer a variety of sports on premium artificial turf with professional facilities.

### Box Cricket

**Short Description:** Fast-paced cricket in an enclosed space. Perfect for urban enthusiasts with 6-8 players per side. Modified rules and soft balls make it accessible and fun.

**Long Description:** Box cricket emerged from India's street and gully cricket culture, driven by increasing urbanization and the scarcity of large playing fields. From makeshift beginnings to structured formats, box cricket has become a beloved urban sport.

Box cricket is a popular, fast-paced version of cricket played in enclosed spaces like indoor courts or netted outdoor areas. It's ideal for areas with limited space as the smaller, contained format uses modified rules and soft balls, making it accessible and fun for casual players.

**Features:**
- 6-8 players per team
- Quick 20-minute matches
- League tournaments available
- Enclosed netted area

### Football / Futsal

**Short Description:** Experience the thrill of football on our premium artificial turf. Professional coaching available to help budding footballers take up the sport as a career.

**Long Description:** The history of football is a long one, with ancient ball games played in cultures like China, Greece, and Rome. Modern football was codified in England with standardized rules, and the sport was introduced to India in the 19th century.

Experience the thrill of football on our premium artificial turf. At Turf 360, we help budding footballers with professional coaches and assistance to take up football as a career. Our facility accommodates both 5-a-side and 7-a-side matches, perfect for casual games or competitive tournaments.

**Features:**
- 5-a-side & 7-a-side formats
- Tournament-ready facilities
- Professional coaching available
- Career guidance for aspiring players

### Pickleball

**Short Description:** The fastest-growing sport in India! Easy to learn, fun to play, and great exercise. Perfect for all ages - as long as you can move around and swing a racket.

**Long Description:** Pickleball originating from its humble roots in 1965 on Bainbridge Island, Washington came to Indian soil around 2008. The sport is on the increase in India because of shifting recreational patterns.

As cities become smaller in size, individuals are seeking sports that do not demand large courts. Pickleball involves racket skills, hand-eye coordination, and strategic gameplay. While perfecting the shots, the true essence lies in the camaraderie it fosters. It welcomes everyone, regardless of age.

**Features:**
- All ages welcome
- Easy to learn, fun to master
- Great full-body workout
- Growing pickleball hub

### Snooker

**Short Description:** A unique combination of snooker in a picturesque setting. Develop fundamental techniques like proper stance, grip, and smooth cue action.

**Long Description:** Snooker originated in the 19th century in colonial India, where British soldiers stationed in the country sought ways to pass the time. The game evolved from traditional billiards and other cue sports.

Snooker skills include fundamental techniques like proper stance, grip, and a smooth, straight cue action with follow-through. Advanced skills involve exceptional cue ball control, positional play, aiming accuracy, and a strong mental game. We offer a unique combination of a snooker table in a picturesque setting.

**Features:**
- Premium quality tables
- Scenic outdoor setting
- Skill development focus
- Strategic gameplay
